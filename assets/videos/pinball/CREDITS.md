# PINBALL trailer

**PINBALL — Lê Anh Khoa.** Current published cut: V5 Portal + Magic Hat, 23 October 2026.

Gameplay, characters and environments were captured from the owner's Unity project, Project C - Pinball. Camera shake, hit stops, slow motion, typography and impact graphics are trailer editing. Sound effects were designed and synchronized to the picture; they are not a direct recording of Unity audio.

V5 replaces two repeated Dreamwalker shots with gameplay captured through Unity MCP: a Portal traversal at 7.50–9.00 seconds (0.67× playback) and a Magic Hat straight-laser activation at 22.2667–25.00 seconds (normal speed). Both were captured at 1920 × 1080 and 60 fps using controlled Play Mode setups and the game's collision, teleport and laser logic. The scene was restored without saving changes. The rest of the edit and the complete Moon Burst audio mix retain their timing; no new Portal or laser sound was added.

## Music and sound design

Music: **Pinball - Playful Precision**, an instrumental generated with Suno through Ace Data Cloud using the owner's approved generation, then edited and mixed locally. The creative target was playful toy-funk at approximately 138 BPM. This publication uses the approved take A with revised music ducking around picture-synchronized effects.

Recorded sound effects used under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/):

- [Cartoon - Duck Quack](https://freesound.org/people/Breviceps/sounds/445960/) — Breviceps.
- [Rubber Duck](https://freesound.org/people/Slothfully_So/sounds/685067/) — Slothfully_So.
- [Cartoon Boing.wav](https://freesound.org/people/reelworldstudio/sounds/161122/) — reelworldstudio.
- [Cartoon Pop](https://freesound.org/people/Mish7913/sounds/741368/) — Mish7913.

Additional impacts, flipper contacts, motion accents, typography cues and the projectile-reflect sequence were synthesized and edited for this production. The retained Moon Burst mix includes 26 ball–obstacle contact cues with original toy, plastic and wood sounds, plus short music ducking. Its redesigned Moon reflect follows the first contact ring at 16.067 seconds, the star expansion at 16.133 seconds and the return flight at 16.367 seconds. Quack bodies and the first strong squeak attacks remain aligned to the unchanged collision shots. The mix measures approximately −17.1 LUFS integrated and −2.1 dBTP. V5 copies the AAC packets unchanged; SHA-256 of their concatenated payload is `1f29d51610852c2489e41876f19da0a4e32343f22bea34d4c7ab6b57535254af`, matching V4 Moon Burst.

Motion design uses the portfolio's palette and typography, with custom EffectCraft impact graphics and a Remotion edit. The game footage was captured in Unity; the music is AI-generated. Raw sound sources, stems and editable production files are excluded from the public build.
